Privacy Policy

Application: Test Device

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Test Device is an Android application designed to help users test and diagnose their device's hardware and functionality. This Privacy Policy explains how information and permissions are handled when you use the application.

2. Information Collection

Test Device does not operate its own server to collect or store users' personal information. Device information displayed during diagnostic tests is used to provide the requested functionality.

Certain tests may access technical information about your device, such as battery status, storage, memory, sensors, and hardware capabilities. This information is used to perform the relevant tests and display their results.

3. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the application may request access to device functions such as the camera, microphone, sensors, vibration, flashlight, or other hardware capabilities.

These permissions are used to perform the corresponding tests. You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related test from working.

4. Internet Speed Test and Third-Party Services

The internet speed test feature uses the Speedtest service to measure network performance. When you use this feature, your device communicates with third-party servers to perform the test.

This connection may involve technical information such as your IP address, network details, and test results, as handled by the third-party service. Such information is subject to that service's own privacy policy and practices.

For more information, please review the Speedtest Privacy Policy .

5. Data Storage and Sharing

Test Device does not sell users' personal information. The application does not send diagnostic information to servers operated by the developer unless this is explicitly disclosed in a future version.

Third-party services used by individual features may process information as necessary to provide their services.

6. Data Security

We recommend keeping your Android operating system and the application updated. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Test Device is a general-purpose device diagnostic utility. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the official contact channel provided with the app.
